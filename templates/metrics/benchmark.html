Scope, Limitations & Factory Validity
Zero False Negatives Context: While Defect Recall is 100.0% (30/30) on this student set, this cannot establish real factory reliability because images are synthetic procedural renderings with fixed centering and dark borders.
Key Operational Constraints
  • Procedural Data: Synthetic metal plate illustrations with simulated scratch/pit textures, not industrial camera photographs.
  • Fixed Framing Contract: Cropped from [16:112, 16:112]; unaligned, rotated, or dirty real-world parts will degrade performance.
  • Uncalibrated Sigmoid Scores: Confidence values are raw logistic outputs, not calibrated safety probabilities.
  • Human Supervision Mandate: Supervisor verification is required for quality audit compliance.
